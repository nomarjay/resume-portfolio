Louie's Car Stereo - Website Redesign Case Study

Louie’s Car Stereo, a local car audio and tint shop in Jacksonville, FL, had an outdated and archived website that hadn’t been updated in over two years. As a result, the business had minimal online visibility and wasn’t reaching its full potential in attracting new customers.

To address this, I took on the task of redesigning the website from the ground up. My goals were to modernize the brand’s online presence, provide updated business information, and make the site more accessible to a wider audience.

    Process

  1. Research & Strategy: I began by evaluating the original website and identifying key areas that needed improvement—content, responsiveness, design, and visibility.
  2. UI Mockup Creation: I designed a new mockup that would serve as a blueprint for the final website. The design is clean, easy to navigate, and tailored to highlight the shop’s services, location, and contact info.
  3. Web Development: Using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I coded the site layout and applied styling consistent with the new branding. I then ensured the site was fully responsive for mobile and tablet devices.
  4. Basic SEO Optimization: To improve discoverability, I added metadata, descriptive alt tags, and keyword-optimized content to help the site rank better in search engines

Style Cutz Barbershop - Website Redesign Case Study

Style Cutz Barber Shop is a well-established neighborhood barbershop in Orange Park, Florida, with deep roots in the local community. I’ve been a loyal client of the shop since its inception, and even before that, I followed the shop’s original owner—my longtime barber—to his own establishment when he first opened Style Cutz.

Over the years, I transitioned to a new barber named Chris, who has now been cutting my hair for nearly a decade. Chris eventually inherited the shop after the original owner sadly passed away due to illness. As a young, ambitious Black business owner, Chris has been navigating both the responsibilities of being a full-time barber and managing the business on his own.

    Process

  1. Understanding the Legacy: Being a longtime client of the shop and a personal friend of the new owner gave me unique insight into the barbershop's evolution, culture, and clientele. My goal was not to erase its history, but to honor it by modernizing it respectfully.
  2. Redesign Approach: I kept the familiar layout while introducing a bold professional hero section with background imagery and strong typography. I added clear content blocks for location, contact, hours of operation, and payment methods. I also included social media icons, interactive map, and stock images as placeholders until real content is provided. I then introduced a grid-based layout and prioritized readability and responsive design
  3. Web Development: Using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I coded the site layout and applied styling consistent with the new branding. I then ensured the site was fully responsive for mobile and tablet devices.
  4. Basic SEO Optimization: To improve discoverability, I added metadata, descriptive alt tags, and keyword-optimized content to help the site rank better in search engines

Rylans Way - Website Mockup Case Study

Rylans Way is a local job coaching and life coaching agency located in Jacksonville, Florida. Rylans Way partners with vocational rehab to assist individuals with disabilities regain independence and secure employment.

Rylans Way needed a website and an online identity to establish its presence. I took on the task of developing a user mockup that highlights the services offered at Rylans Way as well as the contact and location

    Process

  1. Understanding the Mission: I began by immersing myself in Rylan’s Way’s mission and services. I identified the key objectives: promote clarity, compassion, and trust through the site. Since the brand was rooted in community care, the tone and layout had to reflect professionalism without losing its human element.
  2. Design Approach: I created a hero section featuring a touching family moment to instantly connect visitors emotionally. The green gradient background was chosen for its calming and growth-oriented feel. Key sections were mapped out: Who We Are, What We Do, Why Choose Us, and a contact footer with location and email details. Typography and spacing were carefully applied to make the content scannable, while design consistency was maintained through margins, font choices, and imagery.
  3. User Interface Implementation: I built the interface using semantic HTML and clean CSS, structuring the page to be modular and responsive. I placed content blocks in a natural reading flow and utilized fixed-width containers to maintain readability across devices. Navigation was simplified, and buttons were added for service browsing and contact.
  4. Practical Additions & Accessibility: I embedded a Google Maps location for ease of visit and included clearly labeled contact information. Text content was written in plain, accessible language, and color contrast was considered for legibility. These elements ensure that all visitors, regardless of ability, can navigate the site effectively.
